# Changelog

## 1.0 (8 October 2026)
First public version. Earlier versions (0.1 to 0.2.1) were used inside the Echo Learning Initiative and were not published.

Main differences from the internal 0.2.1: three safety flags (safe, sensitive, dangerous); a normative grammar; declared dependencies for every block kind; a confidence ceiling that follows derivation through unscored blocks, with no confidence above 0.90; a lower-bound safety rule with verified human reclassification; holds that propagate; message-ID rules; consent and retention provisions; and removal of claims the project could not support.
